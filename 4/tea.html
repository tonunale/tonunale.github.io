Tea (Camellia sinensis)

First Cultivated: ~2000 BCE

Optimal Climate: Humid subtropical highlands

Water Requirement: 40 to 60 inches annually

Tea began as a medicinal and ritual plant in southwestern China before becoming a daily beverage across East Asia. Terraced tea gardens and careful processing created green, oolong, and black tea styles. The crop later drove global trade networks connecting Asia, Europe, and colonial plantations.

Quick fact Tea began in medicinal use and later became a daily social beverage.
