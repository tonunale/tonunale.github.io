Soybeans (Glycine max)

First Cultivated: ~3000 BCE

Optimal Climate: Temperate to warm temperate

Water Requirement: 18 to 26 inches annually

Soybeans originated in East Asia and became a versatile source of protein and oil. Fermentation produced soy sauce, miso, and tempeh traditions, while tofu and edamame remain everyday foods. As a nitrogen-fixing legume, soy also improved crop rotations in traditional farming systems.

Quick fact Tofu, soy sauce, edamame... one crop used across many traditional East Asian foods.
