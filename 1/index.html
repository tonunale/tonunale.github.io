Batman

Batman is a masked vigilante who first appeared in the comic book series Detective Comics. After experiencing childhood trauma, he trained his mind and body to physical perfection in order to fight crime in Gotham City using advanced technology and detective skills.

  • He relies on his intellect, martial arts training, and engineering skills instead of superhuman powers.
  • His strict moral code prevents him from using lethal force, demonstrating his discipline and commitment to true justice.
  • He uses his massive personal wealth to fund crime-fighting initiatives and improve his city through philanthropy.
